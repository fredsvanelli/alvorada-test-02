Note from the SCH-4 design session: SCH-4 creates the `booking` table in its migration but does not add the `Booking` entity, since nothing used it yet. SCH-6 adds the `Booking` entity (columns `id`, `resource_id`, `start_time`, `end_time`, `status` with default `'active'`) and registers it with `TypeOrmModule.forFeature` in `BookingsModule`.
