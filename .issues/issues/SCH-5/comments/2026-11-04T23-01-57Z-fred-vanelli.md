Note from the SCH-4 design session: the `booking.start_time` and `end_time` columns are Postgres `integer` (int32). `validInterval` must also reject a `start` or `end` outside the int32 range (-2147483648 to 2147483647), so a value like `3000000000` returns `invalid_data` instead of reaching the database as a 500.
